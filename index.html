Who we are

A new-generation grower from India’s flower capital.

Bengaluru sits about 900 metres above sea level, with mild days and cool nights through the year: the conditions roses love. Our estate at Doddaballapur, north of the city, puts that climate to work in modern polyhouses with drip fertigation, overhead misting and close monitoring of humidity and plant stress.

Our team travels to the world’s leading growers and flower trade fairs, and brings back what works. The result is a farm built around one idea: every stem should be good enough to fly.

  • Varieties chosen for vase life, head size and colour that travel well
  • Harvested at the right cut stage, hydrated and cooled the same day
  • Graded by stem length and bunched to each buyer’s specification

Farm to flight

Every stem follows the same careful path

From the polyhouse bed to the cargo terminal, each step protects freshness and vase life.

  1. 01

    Grow

    Polyhouse beds with drip fertigation, misting and daily crop scouting.

  2. 02

    Cut

    Harvested at the right bud stage, early in the cool of the morning.

  3. 03

    Hydrate & cool

    Into clean water and the cold room within hours of cutting.

  4. 04

    Grade

    Sorted by stem length and head size; anything below grade stays home.

  5. 05

    Bunch & pack

    Sleeved and boxed to buyer specification, labelled by variety and length.

  6. 06

    Fly

    Cold-chain transfer to Bengaluru airport, under an hour from the farm.
